What is Bike School?

Part fitness tracker, part social club, part knowledge archive. Built around the content and ethos of Kyle Pounds.

🎧

Podcasts You Earn

New episodes unlock as you log ride time. The archive runs deep — get pedaling and work your way through it.

🗺️

Local Tribes

A tribe leader plants a flag on the map, claims their area, and schedules meetups. Find your people within riding distance.

📊

Strava-Style Metrics

Log your watts, ride weight, incline, and cadence. Your performance feeds directly into your character ranking.

🏆

Character Progression

Two scores: Participation (events attended, activity) and Bike School Progress (podcast milestones reached). Your public profile shows both.

📅

Real-World Meetups

Tribe leaders host rides on their schedule. Attend and earn seniority. Hosts can charge an entry fee or keep it free.

💬

Moderated Community

A tight-knit, heavily moderated space to trade routes, challenge each other's watts, and talk the content you're riding to.

How It Works

Simple loop. Ride, learn, level up, repeat.

1

Create Your Profile

Set up your character. Log your bike specs — weight, geometry, whatever affects your watts.

2

Join or Found a Tribe

Find a local tribe leader and sign up, or claim an unclaimed territory and start your own.

3

Ride & Listen

Queue up Kyle's podcasts, hit the road, and log your ride. The more you pedal, the more you unlock.

4

Show Up & Earn

Attend your tribe's meetups. Each appearance adds to your seniority score and visible rank.

Your Rank Is Earned,
Not Bought.

Every rank in Bike School is a reflection of actual time in the saddle, content consumed, and community participation. No shortcuts.

Inspired by Kyle's roots and ethos — the hierarchy reflects dedication, not dollars.

  • 🌱
    Initiate

  • 🚲

  • ⚡

  • 🔥
    Watt Warrior
    200 ride hours + full season + tribe events
  • 👑
    Tribe Leader
    Claimed territory. Running active meetups.
